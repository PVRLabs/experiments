# Social Media Discussion Policy

I share PVRLabs technical articles and experiments across Reddit, Hacker News, daily.dev, LinkedIn, and other engineering communities.

I publish this work because I enjoy exploring engineering problems, measuring tradeoffs, and sharing practical experience. Most experiments include publicly available methodology, measurements, and reproduction materials.

I appreciate thoughtful discussions, including strong technical disagreement. Participation is voluntary, and I choose where to invest my time.

## Feedback I welcome

- **Technical criticism:** Identify mistakes, questionable assumptions, or limitations in the methodology.
- **Alternative explanations:** Challenge conclusions with technical reasoning.
- **Reproducible evidence:** Share measurements, references, or counterexamples.
- **Real-world experience:** Explain how results might differ under other workloads or constraints.
- **Constructive questions:** Ask about decisions, tradeoffs, or experimental scope, including whether the constraints themselves make sense.

You don't have to agree with my conclusions. Strong disagreement is welcome when supported by reasoning.

## Discussions I won't engage with

### Insults and personal attacks

Derogatory language, personal insults, and attacks on professional competence are not acceptable substitutes for technical arguments.

### Repeated misrepresentation

Misunderstandings happen, and I'm happy to clarify. Repeatedly arguing against claims I haven't made after clarification is rarely productive.

### Dismissal without useful information

Not every experiment needs to discover something new. Reproducing known behavior, measuring tradeoffs, and documenting practical observations can still be valuable to other engineers.

References to established results or explanations of known behavior are welcome. Simply calling an experiment obvious or trivial adds little to the discussion.

### Recommendations that ignore the constraints

Engineering decisions depend on workloads, existing architecture, operational requirements, and practical limitations. Suggestions such as "just rewrite it" or "use a different technology" need reasoning about how they address those conditions.

The constraints are open to challenge too. Explain why a different set of requirements or a different approach would be more useful.

### Personal accusations

Questions about commercial relationships or potential conflicts of interest are reasonable. Unsupported accusations about personal motives don't help evaluate the evidence, methodology, or conclusions.

### Circular arguments

I don't engage indefinitely in repeated accusations or discussions that stop making progress toward understanding the technical issue.

## My engagement policy

I read comments across platforms when I have time, but I don't promise to respond to everyone. I prioritize discussions that contribute useful technical information or help improve future experiments.

I may leave unproductive discussions without explanation. **Personal attacks, insults, and repeated bad-faith behavior may result in an immediate block, without warning or explanation.**

Blocking is a personal decision about how I spend my time, not a judgment of anyone's technical abilities.

## Why I publish

I believe useful engineering knowledge comes from questioning assumptions, measuring behavior, and understanding tradeoffs.

My experiments are not intended to provide universally optimal solutions. They explore specific questions under documented conditions.

I share them to encourage practical, evidence-based engineering discussions.

**Criticize the methods. Challenge the conclusions. Share better evidence. Just leave the personal attacks out of it.**
